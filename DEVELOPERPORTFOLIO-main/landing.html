Why Case Studies Win Interviews

System Architecture

STAGE 03

STAGE 04

Technical Challenges

Discuss difficult bugs, concurrency races, memory limits, and the thoughtful architectural tradeoffs you made.

STAGE 05

Measurable Outcomes
